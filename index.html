One-time setup — Spotify Developer App

Spotify requires this to have its own free "Client ID" before anyone can log in through it. Takes about 2 minutes, and only needs to be done once by whoever is hosting this page.

How to get a Client ID (one-time setup)
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click Create app (or open your existing app if you already made one for this).
  3. For Redirect URI, add exactly this address, then click Add, then Save:
  4. Check the box for Web API, agree to the terms, and click Save.
  5. On the app's page, click Settings and copy the Client ID at the top — paste it above.

Because your app is in Spotify's free "Development Mode," only people you've added by email under the app's User Management section (up to 25) will be able to log in and use this page — add your friends' Spotify account emails there if you want them to try it too.
